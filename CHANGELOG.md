# Changelog

## 2.0.2

Much faster on phones.

- **Fast mode** (on by default on Android; *Awards -> Stats -> Settings -> Fast mode*: Auto / ON / OFF, which also works on a computer): the backdrop (sky, stars, glow round the planet, vignette, the panel's background) is one picture made once instead of several full-screen blends every frame, the side panel is drawn a few times a second (at once when you tap or scroll), and the game runs at 30 frames a second. In the planet view a frame takes about half of what it did on the test computer, and spares a phone's processor the full-screen blends it does slowly.
- **Cheaper frames everywhere:** texts are kept instead of being painted again every frame, asteroids are turned in steps and kept instead of being rotated every frame, the galaxy map keeps its scaled backdrop while the camera does not move (it was the most expensive thing the map did), the fleet's power is worked out once a frame instead of a dozen times, and the 200 achievement conditions are looked at twice a second.
- **A phone's canvas is 1280 pixels along its long side** (1280x576 on a 20:9 screen, a third fewer pixels than before).
- **Show FPS** in the settings, and the five-minute line of the log now says whether fast mode was on.

## 2.0.1

A cleaner black hole at the Void Throne.

- **The black hole looks right.** Its disk is now light that glows (white-hot at the inner edge, red at the outer) over a sky that is darkened round the hole, the bent light hugs the edge of the hole instead of being a big orange blob, the near side of the disk is brighter without a seam, the rings of the bomb pass behind the hole and its beams stop at the edge, so the hole stays black. New screenshots.

## 2.0.0

**The whole galaxy is the game now.** Every world of every star you liberate (about 15,900) is a planet you can settle and play, the war runs on generals and wardens, you attack by dragging on the map, there is a star system view between the galaxy and the planets, and the black hole at the middle of the galaxy can be visited - and turned into a bomb.

- **The Black Hole Bomb.** The Void Throne (Sagittarius A\*, where the Devourer sits) can now be visited once you hold it: its system view is one big black hole - a glowing disk, bent light, the ring at its edge. Build a **bomb** on it in five stages (Mirror Shell, Superradiant Core, Containment Lattice, Spin-up Rings, Trigger Array), each paid in the ore of your richest tier-11 planet, credits and science (the first four come out of what you have saved up when you beat the Devourer, the last takes about eight hours of income), and each one appears round the hole. A finished bomb goes off when you Ascend: **Stardust x2** for that Ascension, shock waves over the screen; it is used up, and an unfinished one is lost. Two new achievements (*Bomb Maker*, *Big Bang*: 202 in all), dev cheats, a `bomb=1` option for the balancing bot, new files `blackhole.py` and `holeview.py`.
- **The Kuiper Belt replaces Proxima b** as the 12th and last planet of the solar system: Proxima b belongs to another star, so the first part of the game is now the solar system only. Same place in the game (price, ore production x3, buildings x1.5, megaproject x5), new look: an ice dwarf world with tholin patches, Tholin ore, the *Comet Catchers* megaproject and a fleet of comets and snowballs (Comet Skiff ... Eris Citadel, Halley Ark) that sweep round the planet on long comet orbits. Saves are unchanged (the Interstellar achievement is now Last Frontier and is kept).
- **Generals and wardens.** The war now runs on standing orders. Liberating one of twelve famous *command systems* (Alpha Centauri, Barnard's Star, Sirius, ... Cygnus X-1) earns you a commander for good: eight **generals** for the offence, four **wardens** for the defence. A general commands an army (a share of the fleet) and carries orders you give once - **where** (a heading on the map, toward a place, clear a region, anywhere) and **under what circumstances** (minimum odds, which factions it may fight and whether it may start a war, what it prefers, when to stop) - and then picks its own targets and runs its own campaigns, offline too. Generals have traits and ranks and pause after two defeats in a row. Pick a direction by clicking on the map; headings show as arrows.
- **Defence separate from offence.** The fleet is split into a defence pool (a slider, 35% to start), the generals' armies and a manual pool for the campaigns you launch yourself (the Send selector and the Command track now work on that pool). Armies defend nothing; the defence never leaves home. Each region where an enemy at war with you touches your border has a posture (Skeleton / Balanced / Fortress) deciding its share of the pool, and a warden multiplies a region's defence and can retake lost systems by itself.
- **The war while you are away.** Campaigns now keep going (and generals keep launching new ones) for up to 8 hours of absence, by the same rules as active play; the enemy still never attacks while the game is closed. The dice are saved: reloading cannot reroll a battle.
- **The Void Throne is about 3x stronger** (a boss-only tuning knob): with the war automated and a defence that holds, the bot-played first Ascension had moved from ~75 to ~47 hours, and this brings it back to 65 - 90.
- The Galaxy tab has three pages (War room, Generals, Defence); new dev cheats for commanders; the balancing bot gives its generals orders and stands the defence down for the Void Throne.
- **The planets of other stars.** Liberating a star now shows what orbits it. The Planets tab has a new *All worlds* page: every world you found (nearest star first), filters for habitable, rocky and giant worlds or for the star you selected, a card with the world's picture and numbers and a *Show on the map* button; the map's bar names the worlds of a system you hold. About 30 real stars (TRAPPIST-1, Proxima Centauri, Kepler-186, Tau Ceti, 55 Cancri...) show their real confirmed planets from the Open Exoplanet Catalogue (89 in all); every other star gets a generated system that fits its kind, the same every time and not stored in the save. Ten kinds of world, each with its own procedural picture; 13 new achievements (*First Light*, *Goldilocks*, *Seven Sisters*, *Planetary Zoo*...).
- **A new way to attack.** Waging war no longer needs a panel: on the galaxy map, **drag from a system you hold to a star** and let go to attack it (an arrow shows the target, your win chance or why it cannot be attacked; a faction at peace asks to be declared war on right on the map). The attack worth launching now is ringed in gold and marked **BEST**: the strongest system of a faction you are at war with that your free fleet beats with 90% odds; **Space** (or the **ATTACK BEST** button) launches it, and says why when there is none. A **campaigns dock** in the corner of the map always shows your fronts, the campaigns closest to arriving with ETA and odds, and the button. Dragging from empty space still pans.
- **The star system view, a third level between the galaxy and a planet.** Press **S** (or the new sun button in the planet strip, the **SYSTEM** chip by the planet's name, **OPEN SYSTEM** on the galaxy map and in the Galaxy tab): the Sun with its twelve planets and the Moon, or any star you hold with all its worlds, drawn on their orbits round the star (the real orbits of TRAPPIST-1, Proxima Centauri and the other real systems; a generated system for the rest; stars drawn by kind: red dwarf, binary pair, red giant, pulsar...). Click a world for its card and a button that travels there, settles or unlocks it; a bar at the top (GALAXY > Sol > Earth) moves between the levels, the arrow keys step through the worlds. New file `sysview.py`.
- **A restyled side panel**: every tab has an icon and its key number, and a header under the tabs names the tab with a one-line summary (and the buy quantity on Ships and Colony). New file `icons.py`.
- New files: `worlds.py`, `worldview.py`, `assets/worlds.json`, `tools/make_worlds.py`.
- **The whole galaxy is the game: settle any world of any star you hold.** The solar system is only the first system now. Every world of every liberated star (about 15,900 in the galaxy) can be settled from the new *Colonies* page of the Planets tab and played like a planet of its own: its own ore, fleet, upgrades, colony buildings and planet bonus, on an economy tier that follows from the strength of its star. A world is paid for in the ore of the two tiers before it (any planet of the tier, solar or not) and costs 6% more for every colony you hold; the first world of each of the ten kinds brings that kind's bonus. *Settle all*, a filter for the selected star and the new **Colonial Office** tech (auto-settle) keep it manageable. The planet strip scrolls and shows the worlds you settled; your planets are a list that is saved and grows (old saves load as before). Colonies are slow-burn by design (10% of the ore and of the fleet strength of a solar planet of their tier, reactors and silos count 2%, banks and labs 1%) so the pace of the game stays what it was; Warp keeps colonies, Ascension takes them. Five more achievements (*Settler* ... *A Home on Every Star*); the balancing bot settles worlds (`col=0` turns it off, `colmax=N` caps it).
- Under the hood: a game keeps its own tables of planets (`g.sites`, `g.sc`, `g.tier`...), `ships.py` is indexed by ship family, `director`, `devtools` and `sim` follow the list of planets; a cache of the upgrade totals makes recalculating many planets cheap.

## 1.1.0

The galaxy is now the real Milky Way with thousands of systems, you can attack several fronts at once, the enemy can take your systems, and every planet has two more ship classes.

- **Two new ship classes: Citadel and Ark** (10 classes now, with a name, description and look on every planet). They are the biggest ships: a Citadel is a flagship with its escorts, an Ark a line of three flagships, both built from the planet's own hulls, drawn larger and leading the fleets on the galaxy map. They cost 6.0B and 60.0B base, mine 110K and 280K ore/s, fight 1.2x better than the class before, and come with upgrades (68 per planet) and two more "Fan" achievements (182 in total). Old saves load with the new classes at zero. The balancing bot still reaches the Void Throne at about 70 hours.
- **Multiple fronts.** Attack several systems at the same time: the new **Command** armory track (7 levels) adds one front per level, up to 8. Each attack sends a share of the fleet that is at home - the smallest force that wins with 95% (*Enough*, the default), 25%, 50% or *All* - so splitting the fleet trades odds for speed; losses count only the ships that were sent. The Galaxy tab has a Fronts card with every fleet in the field, the map shows each fleet on its own lane and each siege, the wide map's top bar lists the next arrivals, and the Fleet Admiral fills every free front by itself. Old saves with a running campaign keep it as one front. New achievements: Two-Front War, Multi-Front Command, War on Every Front.
- **Enemies take your systems.** Counter-offensives now come from every enemy faction at war with you at once (waves of up to three), hit as hard as the system they start from allows (0.8x to 6x the power of the system they attack) and are met by the garrison or by the part of your fleet that is at home - fleets away on a campaign defend nothing. The enemy grows bolder and strikes sooner while your fleets are away.
- The balancing bot fills every front and goes for the Void Throne first; `--selftest` covers fronts, saves and counter-offensives.
- `--new-game` (also `--reset`): start over from the terminal. It asks you to type yes (`--yes` skips the question), erases the save and its backup and starts a new game, keeping a copy of the old save as `.space_miner_save.json.before-reset-DATE`.
- **The real Milky Way as the galaxy map.** The 15 fixed sectors are gone: the map is now the Milky Way seen from above, with **more than 6,000 star systems** to conquer. About 140 are real stars and objects at their real distances and directions (Alpha Centauri, Sirius, Betelgeuse, the Orion and Crab nebulae, Cygnus X-1, Omega Centauri, Sagittarius A*...) and thousands are generated over the real shape of the galaxy. **Travel is free**: you can attack any system within your new **Jump Drive** range (22 levels, 6 light-years to 45,000) of a system you hold. Systems have their own power, faction and reward; the map zooms from the whole galaxy to a single star, with names, lanes, range circles, a ruler and a mini-map; the Galaxy tab has a Jump Drive card and a target browser. Old saves are converted (your sectors become the same stars).
- **Fleet Admiral** (new tech): launches campaigns by itself against the factions you are at war with.
- Campaigns take longer against stronger systems plus the flight; an easy win costs few ships.
- Stardust perks that kept your first sectors now keep your nearest systems (25 / 100). New achievements for the size of your empire, famous places and the Jump Drive.
- `tools/make_galaxy.py` rebuilds `assets/galaxy.json`.
- **A bigger galaxy map with a screen of its own**: a star-chart background, a slim top bar, a map that fills the window (a *wide* layout with a command bar for the selected system, or beside the side panel), incoming-attack chips with Fortify.
- **Every planet's ships look and move differently.** Each planet now has its own design language instead of the same hulls with extras bolted on: silver landers on spindly legs (Moon), wheeled rovers (Mars), airships (Venus), sun-shields and solar wings (Mercury), tumbling rocks with claws (Ceres), striped storm hulls (Jupiter), discs with tilted rings (Saturn), crystals (Uranus), deep-sea creatures (Neptune), ice sleds and hearts (Pluto) and living spores (Proxima b), each in its own colours. They also fly their own paths (hopping, driving over the planet, floating, swinging round a lopsided orbit, spiralling, a flat ring plane, over the poles, weaving, drifting, a figure of eight) and fire their own mining lasers. Prices, output and balance are unchanged.
- `tools/ship_sheet.py` draws a contact sheet of every ship.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

### The game
- Idle mining across 12 planets, each with its own ore, 8 ship types, 58 upgrades and 6 colony buildings; travel between planets, megaprojects, a 30-tech tree, quests, supply crates and 9 kinds of random events.
- Warp (prestige) with Dark Matter and 22 perks; **New Game+** (Ascension) with Stardust and 21 perks.
- The **galaxy**: 15 enemy-held sectors in 6 factions, campaigns, an armory (weapons, armor, engines, shields, intel) and permanent sector bonuses.
- The **Threat Director**: adaptive raids and counter-offensives, Intel warnings, Fortify, faction hostility.
- **Frontline close-up**: a zoomable galaxy map with battle lines, fleets, garrison shields and a mini-map.
- 165 achievements, a stats page, procedural graphics and sound (no asset files for those).

### Release
- Resizable window, fullscreen (F11 / Alt+Enter, `--fullscreen`) that scales to fit any screen.
- **Higher resolutions**: 1280x720, 1600x900, 1920x1080 and 2560x1440 (automatic by default, `--resolution WxH`, a Size button in the settings). The whole layout adapts: a taller panel, a bigger map and a larger asteroid field.
- **Android**: the game plays on phones and tablets (landscape): tap, drag to scroll with momentum, pinch to zoom the galaxy map, back button, saves when you leave the app and pays the offline progress when you return. The canvas takes the shape of the screen. `buildozer.spec` and a release job build the APK.
- Linux build: a one-file executable with an installer and a menu entry; no Python or pygame needed.
- The DejaVu Sans font is bundled, so the game looks the same on every system.
- `--version`, `--help`, `--dev` (cheats, terminal only) and `--selftest` (uses a temporary save).
- **Logging and crash reports**: a rotating log (`~/.local/state/space-miner/space-miner.log`), a crash report file, a stack dump for native crashes, and errors in a single frame are logged without ending your game. The Stats page has an *Open log folder* button.
- Fixed a crash in the frontline close-up (a random number could land exactly on the end of a list, about once in 65,000 laser shots).
- Save safety: a rolling backup, automatic restore of a damaged save, and an unreadable save is copied aside instead of being overwritten.
